Before we start

Useful questions, answered plainly.

If the work sounds familiar, send the current product or idea. We’ll help you find the next sensible move.

Do we need to rebuild everything?

Usually not. The first job is to understand what is safe to retain and where a focused intervention will create the most confidence.

Can you work alongside our existing developers?

Yes. We can provide a clearer product and technical route, then work with your team or take on defined delivery pieces.

What does a software rescue start with?

A short review of the product, repository, deployment set-up and current goals. We then turn that into an honest priority list and a practical next release.
