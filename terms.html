    We agree the work and deliverables before work starts. Per-project packages are for smaller, clearly scoped tasks. Monthly delivery is for ongoing work billed by month. New requirements or work outside the agreed scope may need a new price and timeline.

  3. Prices and payment

    Prices shown on the website are starting prices, not a promise that every project will cost that amount. The final price is the one agreed in writing or shown on your invoice. Payment is due as stated on the invoice, and we start work after payment and scope agreement unless we agree otherwise. We issue invoices through Invoice Ninja in GBP, USD or EUR, with the payment methods available in that portal.

    Monthly delivery is billed in advance for each month. You can ask us to pause or resume it for a future month. Retainer discounts apply only where they are stated in an invoice or separate written agreement. Unused time does not roll over unless we agree otherwise.
