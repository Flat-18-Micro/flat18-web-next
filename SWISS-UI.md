# Flat 18 Swiss UI System

## Intent

Flat 18 uses a Swiss-informed digital product agency style: structured, direct and evidence-led. It is not a historical imitation. The aim is to make complex product work feel clear and dependable in both light and dark modes.

## Layout

- Use a 12-column grid on desktop, 8 columns on tablet and 4 on mobile.
- Keep one shared content edge and gutter across sections.
- Use columns and rules to create hierarchy. Do not use floating cards to create importance.
- Cards are square grid cells where possible. Their borders define the layout.

## Typography

- Inter is the working typeface for headings, copy, controls and data.
- JetBrains Mono is reserved for metadata, indexes, labels and technical detail.
- Colitez Serif italic may highlight a small number of words in a campaign-level heading.
- Dela Gothic is a display voice only. Use it for the primary homepage proposition, not as the default heading face.
- Use weight, size, placement and whitespace before colour to establish hierarchy.

## Colour and surfaces

- The base palette is paper and ink, with one blue accent and a restrained brass accent.
- Use only three surface states: base, lightly tinted and inverted.
- Prefer 1px rules over shadows, blur or gradients.
- Do not use glass effects, glows or decorative colour fields unless they explain product state.
- One campaign-level geometric accent may use colour when it stays contained, remains secondary to the proposition and has a quiet reduced-motion state.

## Motion and interaction

- Motion must reveal structure, progress or a state change.
- Hover states should use a small colour, border or arrow movement—not lift, glow or theatrical animation.
- Respect reduced-motion settings everywhere.

## Content hierarchy

The homepage should follow a simple editorial sequence:

1. Proposition
2. Product situations
3. Proof through work
4. Technology and delivery routes
5. Pricing and contact

The AI-assisted delivery message is stated once clearly, then supported by capability and case-study evidence rather than repeated decorative claims.

The technology panel is the main kinetic element. Its four logo rows move in alternating directions inside a clipped, bordered frame. Other motion should remain quiet so this gesture feels intentional.

## Implementation rules

- Build new marketing sections from the shared Swiss grid tokens.
- Align labels, headings, descriptions and CTAs to grid columns.
- Keep buttons square, direct and consistent.
- Use numbered systems where sequence or comparison helps.
- Test both light and dark modes before release.
