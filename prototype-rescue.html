Before we start

Useful questions, answered plainly.

If the work sounds familiar, send the current product or idea. We’ll help you find the next sensible move.

Can you work with an AI-generated codebase?

Yes. We can review what exists, identify the risky shortcuts and improve the product without assuming that everything needs to be thrown away.

Do we need to rebuild the prototype?

Not always. We first look at the user journey, code, data and deployment path, then recommend what to retain, repair or replace.

What should we share first?

A product or prototype link, the intended users, what currently works and the next release you need to make credible.
